      写作弹药

      作文素材库 · 六主题观点与例子

      每张卡片含 2 条支持、2 条反对、3 个例子，外加 6 个可替换的关键词和一段主体段示范。考场 30 秒定立场：取一条本方观点做首句，一条反方观点做让步，再挑一个例子改写成你熟悉的人或场景；写不满 150 词时，直接用「主体段示范」换掉例子里的名词。

      万能句式工具箱（点开）· 让步 / 现象数据 / 结论建议

      让步句

      • It is true that ..., but this does not mean that ...诚然……，但这并不意味着……
      • Admittedly, ... has its merits, yet it should not be pushed to an extreme.不可否认……有其可取之处，但不应推到极端。
      • While ... may bring short-term benefits, its long-term costs deserve equal attention.虽然……可能带来短期好处，其长期代价同样值得关注。

      现象 / 数据句

      • A recent survey suggests that a growing number of students ...最近一项调查显示，越来越多的学生……
      • In many cities, ... has become so common that few people stop to question it.在许多城市，……已普遍到很少有人停下来质疑。
      • The past decade has seen a striking shift in the way people ...过去十年，人们……的方式发生了显著变化。

      结论 / 建议句

      • The sensible path is to ..., rather than to swing to the opposite extreme.明智的做法是……，而不是走向另一个极端。
      • What matters is not whether we ... , but how we ...关键不在于我们是否……，而在于我们如何……
      • Only by combining personal effort with institutional support can we ...只有把个人努力与制度支持结合起来，我们才能……

      科技

      Technology

      支持 · 2

      • Technology raises efficiency and widens access: a student in a small town can attend lectures once available only in big cities.

        技术提升效率、拓宽机会：小城学生也能听到过去只在大城市才有的课。

      • Digital tools also free people from repetitive work, leaving more time for creation and for caring for others.

        数字工具还把人从重复劳动中解放出来，留出更多时间创作与照顾他人。

      反对 · 2

      • Over-dependence on technology weakens independent thinking and leaves users exposed to privacy risks.

        过度依赖技术会削弱独立思考，并让用户暴露在隐私风险中。

      • When algorithms decide what we read next, convenience quietly narrows the range of ideas we meet.

        当算法决定我们接下来读什么，便利正悄悄收窄我们接触到的观点。

      例子 · 3

      • A student lets AI draft his essay in minutes, yet cannot explain the argument when the teacher asks.

        一名学生用 AI 几分钟生成作文，老师追问时却讲不清论点。

      • A hospital uses AI to read scans faster, but still relies on doctors to judge unusual cases.

        一家医院用 AI 更快读片，但疑难病例仍要靠医生判断。

      • In many cities mobile payment has made cash almost unnecessary, even for street vendors.

        在许多城市，移动支付让现金几乎不再必要，连街边摊贩也是如此。

      efficiency · access · over-dependence · privacy · algorithm · digital divide

      主体段示范（约 80 词，点开）

      Whether technology improves our lives depends less on the tools themselves than on how we use them. On the one hand, digital platforms widen access to education and free us from repetitive work. On the other hand, over-dependence on them weakens independent thinking and exposes users to privacy risks. A student who lets AI write his essay may hand in a perfect paper, yet lose the ability to defend a single idea. The real question, therefore, is not whether to use technology, but how to keep control of it.

      技术是否改善生活，与其说取决于工具本身，不如说取决于我们怎么用。一方面，数字平台拓宽了教育机会，也把人从重复劳动中解放；另一方面，过度依赖会削弱独立思考，并带来隐私风险。让 AI 代写作文的学生也许能交出一篇完美的文章，却失去了为一个观点辩护的能力。因此，真正的问题不是要不要用技术，而是如何保持对它的控制。

      教育

      Education

      支持 · 2

      • Education should train students to question and to solve unfamiliar problems, not merely to memorize answers.

        教育应训练学生质疑与解决陌生问题，而非只记答案。

      • A school that mixes academic study with real projects prepares students for work far better than one that only drills for tests.

        把课堂学习与真实项目结合的学校，比只刷题的学校更能让学生胜任工作。

      反对 · 2

      • Exam-oriented training still gives many students the fairest chance to move up in society, so it cannot simply be abolished.

        应试训练仍为许多学生提供最公平的上升通道，不能简单废除。

      • Reforming the curriculum without changing how students are admitted often leaves teachers trapped between two systems.

        只改课程却不改招生方式，常让教师被困在两套体系之间。

      例子 · 3

      • A class that debates a real campus issue remembers the vocabulary far better than one that recites definitions.

        围绕真实校园议题辩论的班级，词汇记得比背定义牢得多。

      • A university that requires community service finds its graduates more comfortable speaking in public.

        要求参加社区服务的大学发现，毕业生在公开表达上更从容。

      • A village school with only one science kit still sends a student to a national competition, thanks to a teacher who rewards questions.

        只有一套实验器材的乡村学校，因一位鼓励提问的老师，仍把学生送进了全国竞赛。

      critical thinking · exam-oriented · fair chance · engage · curriculum · access

      主体段示范（约 85 词，点开）

      The purpose of education is not to fill students' minds with answers but to teach them how to ask better questions. A class that debates a real campus issue, for instance, remembers its vocabulary far better than one that recites definitions, because language becomes a tool rather than a list. Admittedly, exam-oriented training still offers many students the fairest chance to move upward, and abolishing it overnight would hurt the very people it protects. The sensible path is to keep the fairness of the exam while making room for curiosity.

      教育的目的不是把答案塞进学生脑子，而是教他们提出更好的问题。比如，围绕真实校园议题辩论的班级，词汇记得比背定义牢得多，因为语言成了工具而不是清单。不可否认，应试训练仍为许多学生提供最公平的上升机会，一夜之间废除反而会伤到它保护的人。明智的做法是保留考试的公平，同时给好奇心留出空间。

      环境

      Environment

      支持 · 2

      • Everyday choices send a market signal: when millions of consumers refuse plastic bags, businesses are forced to change.

        日常选择会传递市场信号：当大量消费者拒用塑料袋，企业被迫改变。

      • Cleaner public transport and renewable power can cut emissions without asking citizens to lower their standard of living.

        更清洁的公共交通与可再生能源能在不要求民众降低生活水平的前提下减排。

      反对 · 2

      • Individual guilt can distract attention from the systemic changes that actually cut emissions.

        个人愧疚可能把注意力从真正减排的系统性变革上移开。

      • Strict environmental rules may hit small factories hardest, costing jobs in towns that have few alternatives.

        严格环保规定可能最伤小工厂，让缺少替代出路的城镇失去岗位。

      例子 · 3

      • Imagine a campus bans free plastic bags; within a semester, canteens switch to reusable boxes.

        设想某校园禁用免费塑料袋，一学期内食堂就改用可重复使用的餐盒。

      • A city replaces half its buses with electric ones and records a visible drop in street noise and smog.

        一座城市把一半公交车换成电动，街头噪音与雾霾明显下降。

      • A coastal village restores its mangrove belt and suffers far less damage the next time a typhoon arrives.

        一个沿海村庄修复红树林带，下次台风来袭时损失小得多。

      emissions · systemic · market signal · trade-off · sustainable · resilience

      主体段示范（约 85 词，点开）

      Protecting the environment is often framed as a choice between prosperity and responsibility, yet the two can reinforce each other. When a campus bans free plastic bags, canteens switch to reusable boxes within a semester, and the change costs almost nothing. At the same time, individual effort alone cannot cut emissions at the speed the climate requires; without cleaner power and public transport, personal habits merely ease our conscience. The most effective strategy combines both: policies that change the system, and daily choices that prove the public supports them.

      保护环境常被描述成繁荣与责任之间的取舍，但两者可以相互加强。校园禁用免费塑料袋后，食堂一学期内就改用可重复餐盒，成本几乎为零。但仅靠个人努力无法以气候要求的速度减排；没有更清洁的电力与公交，个人习惯只是让良心好过一点。最有效的策略是两者结合：改变制度的政策，以及证明公众支持的日常选择。

      就业

      Employment

      支持 · 2

      • Choosing a job for growth rather than immediate pay often brings greater rewards in the long run.

        为成长而非眼前薪资择业，长期回报往往更大。

      • A labour market that rewards skills rather than personal connections gives young people from ordinary families a real chance.

        看重能力而非人脉的劳动力市场，才给普通家庭的年轻人真正的机会。

      反对 · 2

      • Under heavy financial pressure, telling graduates to “follow their passion” can be unrealistic and even irresponsible.

        在经济压力沉重时，劝毕业生“追随热情”可能不现实甚至不负责任。

      • When every employer demands three years of experience, the young are locked out of the very jobs that would train them.

        当所有雇主都要求三年经验，年轻人被挡在恰恰能训练他们的岗位之外。

      例子 · 3

      • A graduate takes a lower-paid job that trains him in data analysis, and two years later moves into a stronger role.

        一名毕业生选择薪资较低但能训练数据分析的岗位，两年后转到更好的职位。

      • A factory that pays for evening courses keeps more of its young workers than one that only raises wages.

        为夜校课程付费的工厂，比只涨工资的工厂留住了更多年轻工人。

      • Two classmates with the same degree earn very different salaries five years later, mainly because one kept learning.

        同专业的两名同学五年后薪资差距明显，主要因为其中一人一直在学习。

      long run · trade-off · skills · pressure · experience gap · mobility

      主体段示范（约 85 词，点开）

      Few decisions shape a young person's life as much as the first job, yet the choice is rarely between good and bad. Taking a lower-paid position that trains you in data analysis may look like a loss at graduation, but two years later it can open a stronger role, because skills compound while a starting salary does not. Still, advice to “follow your passion” sounds hollow to a graduate supporting a family. The realistic rule is to accept a job that pays enough today and teaches something for tomorrow.

      很少有什么决定像第一份工作那样影响年轻人的一生，但这个选择往往不是好与坏的二选一。选择薪资较低却能训练数据分析的岗位，毕业时看像吃亏，两年后却可能打开更好的职位，因为技能会累积而起薪不会。不过，对要养家的毕业生说“追随热情”显得空洞。现实的准则是：接受一份今天给得起钱、明天教得会东西的工作。

      文化

      Culture

      支持 · 2

      • Protecting traditional culture gives people a sense of identity and attracts visitors who support local economies.

        保护传统文化给人以认同感，并吸引游客带动地方经济。

      • A tradition kept alive by ordinary people — in food, festivals and crafts — is more convincing than one preserved only in museums.

        由普通人在饮食、节庆与手艺中延续的传统，比只锁在博物馆里的更有生命力。

      反对 · 2

      • Over-commercialization turns living traditions into stage shows performed for tourists.

        过度商业化把活着的传统变成演给游客看的舞台节目。

      • Preserving everything unchanged can freeze a culture that once survived precisely by adapting.

        把一切都原样保存，反而会冻住一个本就靠适应才存活的文明。

      例子 · 3

      • A restored ancient town draws crowds, yet many original residents move out and the streets become souvenir shops.

        修复的古镇引来游客，但许多原住民搬走，街巷变成纪念品商店。

      • A local opera troupe streams short clips online and gains young viewers who then attend live shows.

        一家地方剧团在网上发短视频，赢得年轻观众，他们随后走进剧场。

      • A craft village lets tourists make one simple piece, and its workshop sells more than the gift shop next door.

        一个手工艺村让游客亲手做一件小作品，工坊销量超过隔壁礼品店。

      identity · heritage · commercialization · authenticity · revival · adapt

      主体段示范（约 85 词，点开）

      Culture survives not by being frozen but by being used. A restored ancient town that fills its streets with souvenir shops may attract crowds, yet once its original residents move out, what visitors photograph is a stage rather than a community. By contrast, a local opera troupe that posts short clips online can win young viewers who later buy tickets to a live show. The lesson is that heritage needs both protection and participation: respect the old forms, but let living people keep shaping them.

      文化的存续不是靠被冻住，而是靠被使用。把街巷塞满纪念品店的修复古镇也许能引来人群，可一旦原住民搬走，游客拍下的就只是布景而不是社区。相反，一家在网上发短视频的地方剧团能赢得年轻观众，他们随后会买票看现场。启示是：遗产既需要保护也需要参与——尊重旧的形式，同时让活着的人继续塑造它。

      健康

      Health

      支持 · 2

      • Regular sleep and exercise are not luxuries but the foundation of concentration and academic performance.

        规律睡眠与运动不是奢侈，而是专注与学业表现的基础。

      • Treating mental health as seriously as physical health encourages students to seek help before small problems grow.

        把心理健康与身体健康同等对待，能让学生在小问题变大前就求助。

      反对 · 2

      • Blaming individuals for poor health ignores the structural pressure of long working hours and endless competition.

        把健康问题归咎于个人，忽视了长时间劳动与无休止竞争的结构性压力。

      • An obsession with healthy habits can turn into anxiety, with people measuring every step and every calorie.

        对健康习惯的执念会变成焦虑，人们开始计量每一步、每一卡。

      例子 · 3

      • A student who sleeps an hour more and scrolls less often finds it easier to stay focused in morning classes.

        一名学生多睡一小时、少刷手机后，上午上课更容易保持专注。

      • A company that stops answering emails after 8 p.m. reports fewer sick days within a year.

        一家晚上八点后不再回邮件的公司，一年内病假天数下降。

      • A campus that opens a free counselling room finds most visitors come for ordinary stress, not serious illness.

        一所校园开设免费咨询室后发现，多数来访者为普通压力，而非严重疾病。

      foundation · structural · burnout · habit · preventive · stigma

      主体段示范（约 85 词，点开）

      Health is usually discussed as a personal duty, but it is also a social outcome. A student who sleeps an hour more and scrolls less concentrates better in morning classes, which shows how much daily habits matter. Yet blaming individuals alone is unfair: when a company expects replies at midnight, no amount of discipline can protect its staff from burnout. The reasonable conclusion is that personal habits and working conditions must change together, and that asking for help early should be treated as common sense rather than weakness.

      健康通常被当作个人责任来讨论，但它也是社会结果。多睡一小时、少刷手机的学生上午更专注，这说明日常习惯确实重要。但只怪个人并不公平：当公司期待午夜回复，再强的自律也挡不住员工倦怠。合理的结论是，个人习惯与工作条件必须一起改变，而及早求助应被视为常识而非软弱。

      示例句与段落为原创，方便直接套用；涉及数字、机构与具体案例时，请替换成你自己核实过的材料，不要原样照搬。

      进阶建议

      更高效的练习方式

      1. 先做真题再看解析

      独立完成整套试卷，再对照解析复盘错题，避免“看答案就会”却依旧失分。

      2. 听力分层训练

      第一遍抓主旨，第二遍补细节，第三遍跟读复述，逐步提高理解与输出能力。

      3. 记录高频易错点

      把高频词汇、长难句、写作亮点集中到笔记区，形成专属资料库。

      4. 保持稳定节奏

      建议每天至少完成一套题或一个专项练习，打卡区勾选任务维持连续性。
